## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Launch Verdict: **NO-GO**

RoomLine demonstrates unusually strong foundational engineering in several core areas: atomic PL/pgSQL booking and settlement transactions (`create_booking_atomic`, `settle_online_payment_atomic`, `settle_online_group_payment_atomic`), integer minor-unit currency conversion (`lib/money.ts`), advisory-locked room-type inventory guards (`assert_room_type_inventory`), replay-safe webhook/verify idempotency, and PII-scrubbed Sentry telemetry.

However, **production launch must be blocked** until **6 BLOCKER** and **10 HIGH** findings are resolved. Launching in the current state will cause immediate remote-code-execution / middleware-bypass exposure from known critical CVEs, broken Paystack splits whenever a small platform fee is configured, 100% broken Payouts views, corrupted Night Audit cash/card drawer reconciliations, unauthorized direct PostgREST tampering with booking payment states and hotel subaccounts, and live checkout failures due to test-mode data in the production database.

### Architecture Summary (Verified in Phase 0)
- **Framework & Runtime:** Next.js `16.3.0` (App Router, Turbopack build, `proxy.ts` convention), React `19.2.4`, TypeScript `7.0.2`, `pnpm@10.32.1`, Zod `^4.4.3`.
- **Dual Authentication & Multi-Tenancy Model:**

  2. **Platform Super-Admins (`/platform`):** Supabase Auth (`@supabase/ssr` `^0.12.0` cookie session in `proxy.ts:35-77` and `lib/supabase/platform-server.ts`), authorized against `public.platform_admins` via `assertPlatformAdmin()` (`app/platform/(protected)/_guard.ts:14-33`) and executed via `supabaseAdmin` (`service_role`).
  3. **Guests (`/[slug]`, `/api/booking/[slug]/**`):** Unauthenticated public routes rate-limited via Upstash Redis (`lib/rate-limit.ts`) and executed server-side via `supabaseAdmin`.

### Top 5 Production Risks
1. **7 Critical Runtime Dependency CVEs (Unauthenticated RCEs & Middleware Bypass) [`BLK-01`]:** `next@16.3.0` is vulnerable to multiple unauthenticated RCEs in Image Optimization (including AVIF RCE `GHSA-2xp9-vwfh-vxw4` while `.avif` objects already exist in public `hotel-assets` storage, SVG RCE `GHSA-2464-fhr4-w9xw`, and `next/og` RCE `GHSA-vcvr-r3jv-pc5j` with `app/opengraph-image.tsx` present), and `@sentry/nextjs@10.72.0` is vulnerable to Critical Middleware Bypass (`GHSA-mchm-gjx6-7h55`) when `tunnelRoute: "/monitoring"` is enabled (`next.config.ts:105`).
2. **Paystack Split Fee-Bearer Misconfiguration & Checkout Failure [`BLK-02`]:** Subaccounts are created with `percentage_charge: 0` and transactions omit `bearer` (`lib/paystack.ts:178-182`). Contrary to the code comment, hotels bear Paystack's 1.95% fee when platform fee is `0%`, and setting **any** platform fee between `0.01%` and `1.94%` (or flat fee `< 1.95%` of booking total) shifts the entire 1.95% Paystack fee onto RoomLine's smaller `transaction_charge`, causing Paystack `POST /transaction/initialize` to reject checkout with `400 Bad Request`.
3. **Missing PostgREST Column Guards Allow Staff to Forge Paid Bookings & Hijack Hotel Subaccounts [`BLK-03`]:** Browser clients hold an `authenticated` Clerk JWT (`lib/supabase/client.ts:30-58`). Because `public.bookings` and `public.hotels` lack column-guard triggers (unlike `staff_profiles` and `notifications`), any `front_desk` clerk can `PATCH /rest/v1/bookings` to set `payment_status = 'paid'` / `total_amount = 0.01` with zero payment, and any `manager` can `PATCH /rest/v1/hotels` to swap `paystack_subaccount_code` to an unverified account or unsuspend a suspended hotel.
4. **Live Production Database Contains Test-Mode Paystack Subaccount & Seed Data + Committed Worker Secret [`BLK-04`, `BLK-05`]:** Remote DB `wtgldgyxtpbzjmaygeif` currently holds an active hotel (`sweet-garden-rska`) with a test-mode `paystack_subaccount_code` (`ACCT…`) that will fail 100% of checkouts once `PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY` switches to `sk_live_…`, alongside 80 test bookings and 72 test payments. Additionally, `PUSH_WORKER_SECRET` (`22fd…`) is committed in plaintext in `supabase/migrations/20260821170005_schedule_push_delivery.sql:1` and remains active in production `vault.secrets`.
5. **100% Broken Payouts View, Misclassified Online Payments in Night Audit, & Broken `.env.example` Email Header [`BLK-06`, `HIGH-01`, `HIGH-02`]:** `listSettlements` and `listSettlementTransactions` (`lib/paystack.ts:380-383, 418-421`) `break` out of their pagination loop straight into an unconditional `throw new Error(...)` (missing `return all;`), breaking Payouts 100% of the time. Meanwhile, `settle_online_payment_atomic` hardcodes `method = 'card'` instead of `'paystack_link'`, causing Night Audit and Payment Activity to report GHS 0.00 online collections and overstate front-desk card collections.

## 2. PAYSTACK GROUND TRUTH (Fetched Official Documentation)

All Paystack behavior below was verified directly from official documentation during Phase 1 prior to grading code:

1. **Initialize Transaction & Subunits** ([Transactions API](https://paystack.com/docs/api/transaction/), [Supported Currency](https://paystack.com/docs/api/#supported-currency)):
   - `POST https://api.paystack.co/transaction/initialize` requires server-side `Authorization: Bearer sk_...`, `email`, and `amount` in **subunits** (`GHS × 100` = integer pesewas; minimum transaction for `GHS` is **₵0.10 = 10 pesewas**). Note: `XOF` has no fractional circulation unit yet Paystack **requires** multiplying `XOF` by `100`.
   - Optional parameters: `currency` (`"GHS"`), `reference` (unique per attempt; only `[A-Za-z0-9.= -]`, case-insensitive uniqueness per environment), `callback_url` (fully qualified URL), `metadata` (JSON object), `channels` (`card`, `mobile_money`, `bank_transfer`, etc.), `subaccount` (`ACCT_...`), `transaction_charge` (integer subunits), `bearer` (`"account"` default or `"subaccount"`), `split_code` (`SPL_...`).
2. **Subaccounts & Ghana Bank/MoMo Resolution** ([Subaccounts API](https://paystack.com/docs/api/subaccount/), [Verify Account Number](https://paystack.com/docs/identity-verification/verify-account-number/), [Payment Channels](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/payment-channels/), [Getting Your Money](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2125314)):
   - `POST /subaccount` requires `business_name`, `bank_code` / `settlement_bank`, `account_number`, and `percentage_charge` (float: percentage **the main account receives**). `PUT /subaccount/:id_or_code` updates `business_name`, `description`, `bank_code`, `account_number`, `active` (`true`/`false`), `percentage_charge`.
   - Ghana settlement rails (`GET /bank?country=ghana` or `?currency=GHS&type=mobile_money`) include `ghipss` banks and `mobile_money` wallets (`MTN`, `VOD`/Telecel, `ATL`/ATMoney). `GET /bank/resolve?account_number=...&bank_code=...` is supported and free in Nigeria and Ghana.
   - **Critical Operational Rules:** Paystack disclaims liability for payouts to wrong account numbers. **The first payout to any new or updated subaccount is delayed indefinitely** until verified in the Paystack Dashboard (`Subaccounts → Verify Subaccounts`). If a subaccount with a pending payout is deactivated/deleted, its pending payout freezes until re-enabled (it does **not** divert to the main account) ([Transaction Splits Support](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2132802)).
3. **Split Math & Fee Bearer Semantics** ([Split Payments](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/split-payments/), [Multi-split Payments](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/multi-split-payments/), [Transaction Splits Support](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2132802), [Transactions Pricing](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2130306)):
   - **Ghana Processing Fee:** **1.95%** across local channels (`card`, `mobile_money`, `bank_transfer`) with no flat fee.
   - By default, `percentage_charge`% goes to the **main account** and `(100 - percentage_charge)`% goes to the **subaccount**. Passing `transaction_charge` (subunits) overrides `percentage_charge`: the **main account receives the flat `transaction_charge`** and the **subaccount receives `amount - transaction_charge`**.
   - **Who Bears Paystack Fees (`bearer`):**
     - Default (`bearer` omitted or `"account"`): Paystack charges the **main account**—**EXCEPT** when the main account receives a `0%` / zero deduction, in which case Paystack automatically charges the **subaccount** ([Transaction Splits Support](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2132802)).
     - `bearer: "subaccount"`: Paystack deducts the 1.95% processing fee from the **subaccount's share**.
     - **Invalid Split Rule:** If the fee-bearing party's share is less than Paystack's processing fee, Paystack returns **`400 Bad Request`**.
4. **Verification & Webhooks** ([Verify Payments](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/verify-payments/), [Webhooks](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/webhooks/), [API Keys](https://paystack.com/docs/developer-tools/api-keys/)):
   - `GET /transaction/verify/:reference`: `response.status` is only the HTTP call boolean; `response.data.status === "success"` is the transaction status (alongside `abandoned`, `failed`, `ongoing`, `pending`, `processing`, `queued`, `reversed`). MoMo STK push authorizations allow **180 seconds**; Pay with Transfer allows **15 minutes to 8 hours**.
   - **Webhook Signatures:** Paystack now supports `Paystack-Signature: t=<unix>,v1=whk_<id>:<hex>` signed with a dedicated `whsec_...` secret over `t + "." + rawBody` (300s replay window), while continuing to send `x-paystack-signature` (128-char lowercase hex `HMAC-SHA512` over the **raw unparsed body** using `PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY`, verified with `crypto.timingSafeEqual`) as long as a secret key exists.
   - **Webhook Retries, Timeouts & IPs:** Must return `200 OK` quickly (30s attempt timeout; 5s ping timeout). Non-200 responses retry **every 3 minutes for 4 tries, then hourly for 72 hours in live mode** (hourly for 10 hours in test mode). Official source IPs (both test and live): **`52.31.139.75`**, **`52.49.173.169`**, **`52.214.14.220`**.
5. **Settlements, Refunds, Disputes & Mode Isolation** ([Settlements API](https://paystack.com/docs/api/settlement/), [Automated Payouts](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2123586), [Refunds](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/refunds/), [Initiating a Refund](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2127106), [Manage Disputes](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/manage-disputes/)):
   - **Ghana Settlement:** Automatic **T+1** (next working day; excludes weekends/public holidays). Minimum automatic payout to a Ghana **bank account** is **GHS 50** (amounts below GHS 50 accumulate at no cost; manual request below GHS 50 costs GHS 5; MoMo wallets have no GHS 50 floor). Starter Business lifetime limit in Ghana is **GHS 30,000**. Paystack emits **no** settlement webhook (`GET /settlement?subaccount=<numeric_id>` must be polled).
   - **Refunds & Disputes:** `POST /refund` (`transaction`, optional `amount` in subunits, optional idempotent `reference` 8–64 chars). Paystack's 1.95% processing fee is **non-refundable**. Refunds are deducted from pending payouts or Paystack Balance (Registered Businesses only); customer receipt takes 3–10 working days. Webhook events: `refund.pending`, `refund.processing`, `refund.needs-attention`, `refund.processed`, `refund.failed`, and `charge.dispute.create`, `charge.dispute.remind`, `charge.dispute.resolve` (unanswered chargebacks **auto-accept after 16 hours**).
   - **Mode Isolation:** Test (`sk_test_`/`pk_test_`, `domain: "test"`) and live (`sk_live_`/`pk_live_`, `domain: "live"`) subaccount codes, transactions, webhooks, and refunds are completely isolated.

### 3.2 Worked Money-Split Example: **GHS 1,000.00 Booking** (`100,000` pesewas)

**Code Configuration Inspected:**
- Subaccount creation (`app/actions/paystack.ts:108`, `lib/paystack.ts:146`): `percentage_charge: 0`
- Initialize call (`app/api/booking/[slug]/bookings/route.ts:493-499`):
  - `amount: 100000` (`GHS 1,000.00` via `toMinor(1000, "GHS")`)
  - `currency: "GHS"`
  - `subaccount: hotel.paystack_subaccount_code` (`"ACCT_..."`)
  - `...(platformFeeMinor > 0 ? { transaction_charge: platformFeeMinor } : {})`
  - `bearer`: **`undefined` (NEVER PASSED)**
- Live DB `public.platform_settings` (`id = 1`, queried via MCP): `fee_type = 'percentage'`, `fee_percent = 0`, `fee_flat_minor = 0`, `fee_flat_currency = 'GHS'`.
- Paystack Ghana Local Processing Fee ([Transactions Pricing](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2130306)): **1.95%** (`0.0195 × 100,000 = 1,950` pesewas = **GHS 19.50**).

Because `bearer` is omitted in code, **three radically different behaviors** occur depending on what Platform Admin sets in `/platform/settings`:

| Scenario | `platform_settings` Config | `transaction_charge` Sent | Gross Split Before Paystack Fee | Who Bears Paystack's GHS 19.50 (1.95%) Fee? | **Net to Hotel Subaccount** | **Net to RoomLine Main Account** | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Case 1 (Current Live DB)** | `0%` (`ZERO_FEE`) | *Omitted* (`percentage_charge: 0` applies) | Hotel: GHS 1,000.00 (`100,000p`)<br>RoomLine: GHS 0.00 (`0p`) | **Hotel Subaccount** *(per [Paystack Split Support](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2132802): when main account gets 0%, subaccount bears fee—contradicting `lib/paystack.ts:179`!)* | **GHS 980.50** (`98,050p`) | **GHS 0.00** (`0p`) | Succeeds, but code comment in `lib/paystack.ts:179` is wrong. |
| **Case 2 (Small Platform Fee, e.g. `1.0%` or `GHS 10` flat)** | `1%` (`fee_percent = 1.00`) | `1000` pesewas (GHS 10.00) | Hotel: GHS 990.00 (`99,000p`)<br>RoomLine: GHS 10.00 (`1,000p`) | **RoomLine Main Account** *(default `bearer: "account"`)* — requires `1,950p` fee out of `1,000p` share! | **GHS 0.00 (Checkout Fails)** | **GHS 0.00 (Checkout Fails)** | **`400 Bad Request`!** Paystack rejects `initializeTransaction` (`1,000p < 1,950p` fee) ([Split Docs](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/split-payments/#bearer-of-transaction-fee)). |
| **Case 3 (Platform Fee `5.0%`, Current Code `bearer` omitted)** | `5%` (`fee_percent = 5.00`) | `5000` pesewas (GHS 50.00) | Hotel: GHS 950.00 (`95,000p`)<br>RoomLine: GHS 50.00 (`5,000p`) | **RoomLine Main Account** absorbs the **entire** GHS 19.50 fee (`5,000p - 1,950p`)! | **GHS 950.00** (`95,000p`) | **GHS 30.50** (`3,050p`) *(while DB `payments.platform_fee` logs GHS 50.00!)* | Succeeds, but RoomLine silently loses 39% of its commission to Paystack fees. |
| **Reference: Explicit `bearer: "subaccount"` at `5.0%` Fee** | `5%` + `bearer: "subaccount"` | `5000` pesewas (GHS 50.00) | Hotel: GHS 950.00 (`95,000p`)<br>RoomLine: GHS 50.00 (`5,000p`) | **Hotel Subaccount** bears GHS 19.50 (`95,000p - 1,950p`) | **GHS 930.50** (`93,050p`) | **GHS 50.00** (`5,000p` exact) | Works for any fee `0%` up to `~98%` without `400` errors, and `payments.platform_fee` matches RoomLine's exact net payout. |

> **Flag for Business Owner Review:** Confirm whether hotels (`bearer: "subaccount"`) or RoomLine (`bearer: "account"`, which requires enforcing `platformFeeMinor >= ceil(totalMinor * 0.0195)` whenever `platformFeeMinor > 0`) are intended to absorb Paystack's 1.95% processing fee.
